# fireSense_report (development version)

* First version (0.0.0.9000). Reads a `runs` table (one row per arm, role `main` or `appendix`) and writes four tables per arm and ELF as csv and `sim$reportTables`: parameter sets (median and 2.5-97.5% over the kept sets; year-effect sds), in-sample fit per stage (ignition and escape from `yearTotals`, spread from `inSampleSizes_spread`, NA rows with a note when that column is absent), hindcast with drawn year effects and with fitted year effects (skill rows and the bias rows, test B, of `wave1_results.csv`; NA rows with a note when the score has none). A national summary gives the median and 2.5-97.5% range over ELFs, with `nELF` and `nNA`. The year-effect prior sd is read with `fireSenseUtils::readYearEffects()`, the ledger column name is `fireSenseUtils::spreadFitInSampleSizesTxt`. `report.html` has sortable tables and figures (via `Plots()`: yearly totals, fitted fire sizes and annual area, bias by ELF) in a main and an appendix section.
