# fireSense_report (development version)

* First version (0.0.0.9000). Reads a `runs` table (one row per arm, role `main` or `appendix`) and writes four tables per arm and ELF as csv and `sim$reportTables`: parameter sets (median and 2.5-97.5% over the kept sets; year-effect sds), in-sample fit per stage (ignition and escape from `yearTotals`, spread from `inSampleSizes_spread`, NA rows with a note when that column is absent), hindcast with drawn year effects and with fitted year effects (skill rows, and bias from test B or derived from gates G2, G4, G6 with single-run ranges). A national summary gives the median and 2.5-97.5% range over ELFs. `report.html` has sortable tables and figures (via `Plots()`: yearly totals, fitted fire sizes and annual area, bias by ELF) in a main and an appendix section.
